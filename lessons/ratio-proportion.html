Section 01 // Core Concept

Sharing and scaling

A ratio like 3 : 2 means for every 3 parts of one thing, there are 2 parts of another. The total is 3 + 2 = 5 parts, and you divide the whole into those parts.

Simplifying ratios

Divide both sides by their HCF.
12 : 8 → divide by 4 → 3 : 2. Always reduce to lowest terms.

Sharing in a ratio

Add the parts, find one part, multiply.
£60 in ratio 2:3: total parts = 5, one part = £12. Shares: £24 and £36.

Direct proportion

As one quantity increases, the other increases by the same factor. Double x → double y. y = kx (k is constant).

Inverse proportion

As one increases, the other decreases. Double x → halve y. y = k/x. More workers → less time.

Section 03 // Real Life

Ratios in the wild

🍕

Sharing food

Split 24 slices 3:1 between two people → 4 parts total, one part = 6 slices → 18 and 6.

🗺️

Map scale

Scale 1:50,000 means 1 cm on the map = 50,000 cm = 500 m in real life. Direct proportion.

🏗️

Workers & time

3 workers take 8 hours → 6 workers take 4 hours. Double workers → halve time: inverse proportion.

🧪

Mixing solutions

Squash at ratio 1:4 (concentrate : water). For 500 ml total: 100 ml + 400 ml.

Section 04 // Encrypted: GCSE Stretch

Advanced data

🔒 3-part ratios, best value & inverse proportion
3-part ratios: e.g. share £120 in ratio 2:3:5 → total parts = 10, one part = £12 → shares are £24, £36, £60.
Best value / unit rate: divide total cost by quantity for each option. The lower cost per unit is better value. e.g. 400 g for £1.20 vs 650 g for £1.82 → £3.00/kg vs £2.80/kg → 650 g wins.
Inverse proportion equation: if y is inversely proportional to x then y = k/x. Find k from a given pair (x, y), then use it to find any other value.

Graphical clue: direct proportion gives a straight line through the origin. Inverse proportion gives a curved hyperbola.
